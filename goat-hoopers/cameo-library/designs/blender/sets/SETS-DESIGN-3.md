# SETS DESIGN — STEP 2c: GROUP LIVING SPACES + TWO NEW SETS

*Status: DESIGN SHEETS for review — nothing is built in Blender yet. Same step discipline as Steps 2/2b: approve the designs first, build later on the Mac. Six sets this round: the four group living spaces Aidan asked for ("more sets **like the clubhouse**, sized for more people"), plus the two named in the steering — the outdoor pickup court and the locker room.*

*How this round answers the steering: (1) **DETAIL DENSITY UP** — every set carries a named dressing list of 12–20 props, and the renders show them (the patio table has its cooler open and plates stacked; the draft table is laid with card stacks, mugs and bowls; the locker benches are buried in towels, duffels and sneakers). (2) **SCENE-FIRST** — every set names the specific bits it exists to host, and at least one feature per scene is designed into the architecture (a dive lane, a storm-through gate, a podium bench, a reach-across island).*

*Companions: SETS-DESIGN.md (clubhouse canon: palette hexes, materials, ban list), SETS-DESIGN-2.md (Step 2b studio sets), BLENDER-DESIGN.md (characters + Chef Hiram §3.9b), RIG-DESIGN.md (rig + 45 cm rule). Character work untouched.*

## 0. SHARED RULES (all six sets)

- **SEAT STANDARD (show-wide, from RIG-DESIGN Step 1):** every seat — couch, chair, stool, bench, picnic bench — is modeled at 47 cm so it compresses/sits at **45 cm loaded**. One number, every set, no exceptions. Standing eye-lines and the capybara bed (Ø 70, cushion top ~20) unchanged.
- **Palette (carried from the clubhouse, verbatim):** navy `#1F2A44` · red `#C1272D` · warm wood `#8A5A2B` · cream `#F5F2EC` · grey-blue wall/siding `#7787A0` · brass/gold lamp metal `#C8952F` · black screen/board planes `#101014`. New materials this round, all derived: asphalt `#43434E`, chain-link silver `#AAAEB6`, lawn green `#7A9A52`, locker cream (the cream, on doors) + locker navy (the navy, on frames).

Court = TWO states designed and rendered: GOLDEN HOUR (wide/hoop sheets) and STREETLIGHT NIGHT (bench sheet) — same geometry, lighting swap only.

## 1. THE WATCH-PARTY DEN (`den-`)

**The clubhouse's bigger sibling: the whole league watches the game here.** Interior **960 W × 620 D × 300 H**. North wall = screen wall; sectional faces it; kitchen door on the east wall (this house flows: den ↔ kitchen island set).

**Scenes it exists to host:**
1. **"The Whole League Yells at the TV"** — all-cast reaction pileup on a buzzer-beater. *Designed feature:* the **reaction pyramid** — sectional (6) + floor-cushion front row (3) + the standing lane behind, three tiers of bodies in one CAM C frame.
2. **"The Whisper, Amplified"** — the Lord Lax whisper gag with a live audience: Lax + Vann take the **chaise corner** (the gag's home base), the rest of the sectional as a bank of reactors leaning in.
3. **"Snack Raid"** — two managers contest the sideboard at halftime; a third **dives over the sectional back** out of the standing lane. *Designed feature:* the **DIVE LANE** — 100 cm of clear floor behind the sectional, sectional back height 95: dive-over-able, on purpose.

**Furniture & dressing (density pass):** ① sectional, navy, 6 seats + chaise return (E end) ② loveseat, navy, 2 seats, angled ~20° off the west wall ③ floor cushions ×3 (red center, navy flanks), Ø 80 ④ coffee table 160 × 80 at 40 H ⑤ screen plane 240 × 135 (blank), center 150, on the north wall ⑥ media console 340 × 45 under it ⑦ snack sideboard 240 × 50 at 90 H on the east wall ⑧ blue cooler on the floor at the sideboard's south end ⑨ pizza boxes ×5 stacked on the sideboard (plain) ⑩ popcorn bowl, pretzel bowl, chip bowl + cans ×6 on the sideboard; second bowl set on the coffee table ⑪ game controllers ×2 (sideboard + console) ⑫ coaster stack ⑬ red throw cushions ×5 across sectional + loveseat ⑭ navy throw blanket draped over the sectional back ⑮ trophy shelves ×2 on the north wall flanking the screen (cups plain, unengraved — see sheet note) ⑯ pennant, navy/red, east of the screen ⑰ floor lamp, brass, NW corner ⑱ rug 560 × 330, cream/navy border ⑲ capybara bed Ø 70 by the loveseat ⑳ windows ×2 with blinds, west wall.

**Seating: 11 + standing.** Sectional 6 + loveseat 2 (seat plane **45**) + floor cushions 3 (sit ~15). Dive lane holds 3–4 standing. **This is the full-cast interior.**

**Cameras:** A — MASTER: south, on the (wild) south wall line, room + screen glow. B — FACES CAM: beside the screen, low, down the sectional (reaction singles). C — SCREEN REVERSE: behind the sectional backs, screen + the pyramid in one frame. D — SNACK RAID: low at the sideboard's south end, down its length.

**Sheet notes:** den-sideboard's trophy plaques carry faint engraved-plate texture — **builds keep plaques BLANK** (clubhouse canon: trophies smooth, unengraved). In den-screen the loveseat angle and cushion row drift slightly vs the wide; wide + plan are the contract.

## 2. THE BACKYARD PATIO (`patio-`)

**Golden-hour-to-dusk exterior behind the same house.** Site **1100 W × 800 D**; wood deck **880 × 560** against the house's north siding wall; lawn + fence beyond. Sliding door (180, centered E-of-middle) connects to the interior world.

**Scenes it exists to host:**
1. **"Chef Hiram's Grill Court"** — Hiram (Chef Hiram variant, BLENDER-DESIGN §3.9b) holds court from the grill station, handing down rulings between flips; supplicants approach the prep counter like a bench. *Designed feature:* the **grill station + prep counter** as a judge's bench, set apart at the deck's west end with its own camera.
2. **"The Picnic-Table Summit"** — eight managers around the table negotiate a trade that collapses; the snack board in the middle is the reach-across slapstick zone. *Designed feature:* table 300 long with benches both sides and the **cooler at the east end** — the last-can dive launches off the bench onto the lawn.
3. **"The Slider Entrance"** — anyone can storm in/out through the sliding door mid-argument, string lights swinging. *Designed feature:* the door axis runs straight down the table (CAM B) — entrances land at the head of the summit.

**Furniture & dressing:** ① picnic table 300 × 110 at 75 H ② benches ×2, full length, both sides ③ grill station 150 × 70 at 90 H against the house wall (lid, side shelf) ④ prep counter 120 × 60 beside it ⑤ utensil crock + spatulas ×2 + tongs on the counter ⑥ grill towel draped on the grill handle (Hiram's robe) ⑦ red cooler at the table's east end (open in patio-table) ⑧ popcorn bowl + pretzel plate + cans ×4 on the table ⑨ paper-plate stack (plain) ⑩ string lights ×2 strands zig-zag over the deck ⑪ tiki torches ×2 at the deck's south corners ⑫ deck chairs ×2 on the lawn (SE) ⑬ basketball resting against the fence ⑭ garden hose coil by the sliding door ⑮ fence, wood slats, all round, gate on the south side ⑯ lawn + soft tree/hedge shapes beyond (silhouette dressing only).

**Seating: 8 at the table** (4 + 4 on the benches, seat plane **45**) + 2 deck chairs (seat ~40, lawn furniture — the standard's one noted exception, flagged for Step 3 confirmation) + standing room on deck and lawn.

**Cameras:** A — MASTER: from the lawn, south, through the gate. B — TABLE AXIS: at the sliding door, straight down the table. C — GRILL COURT: at the grill's west side, Hiram's bench + first supplicant. D — TABLE REVERSE: SE lawn corner, summit from the cooler's end.

**Sheet notes:** the lawn/foliage in the sheets reads slightly more photographic than the interiors — builds keep the clubhouse's flat-color foliage shapes (2b arena tree rule). The sliding door's interior glimpse in patio-table is a glow, not a modeled room.

## 3. THE DRAFT TABLE (`draft-`)

**The whole league around one table. Social, loud, not a boardroom.** Interior **840 W × 620 D × 300 H**. Table at center, long axis E–W; board on the north wall; **presenter lane** (100 cm) between table and board.

**Scenes it exists to host:**
1. **"Draft Night Meltdown"** — all 10 seated, one manager standing in the presenter lane at the board, agonizing while nine heckle. *Designed feature:* the **presenter gap** — standing room at the board with the whole table in frame behind (CAM C shoots over the presenter's shoulder; CAM B down the axis).
2. **"The Reach"** — a manager lunges across the table for the last slice / the card stack. *Designed feature:* the table is **150 wide on purpose** — just too wide to reach; the center line of the tabletop is the lunge's point of no return.
3. **"The Sideboard Confessional"** — between picks, losers drift to the snack sideboard and deliver dejected asides to CAM D while the table roars behind them. *Designed feature:* the sideboard nook on the east wall has its own light pool and camera, acoustically "outside" the room.

**Furniture & dressing:** ① draft table 420 × 150 at 75 H, dark warm wood ② chairs ×10 (4 + 4 + 1 + 1), wood, navy seat cushions ③ board plane 300 × 120 (blank), center 165, north wall ④ pendant lights ×3, brass, over the table at 210 ⑤ sideboard 220 × 50 on the east wall — the confessional ⑥ card stacks at the place settings (plain, blank) + a deck box ⑦ mugs ×3 on the table ⑧ popcorn/pretzel/chip bowls on the table + sideboard ⑨ cans ×6 across both ⑩ pizza boxes ×3 stacked at the table's east end ⑪ **the Commissioner's gavel** on its own small side table at the board's west end (Muse runs the draft; the gavel is a character) ⑫ pennant east of the board ⑬ rug 560 × 300 under the table ⑭ window with blinds, west wall ⑮ spare chair stacked in the SW corner (someone always brings a chair).

**Seating: 10** — every manager, chairs at the **45** standard. Eleventh position: the presenter, standing in the lane.

**Cameras:** A — MASTER: SW corner. B — DOWN-TABLE: east end, on the table axis, board beyond. C — PRESENTER/BOARD: south-center, over the table to the lane. D — CONFESSIONAL: SE, tight on the sideboard nook.

**Sheet notes:** draft-wide predates the density pass — its tabletop is bare; **the dressed tabletop in draft-table.png is the dressing contract** (card stacks, mugs, bowls, pizza boxes are canonical). In draft-board the room reads rotated (board on the end wall); wide + plan are the contract — board on the NORTH wall.

## 4. THE KITCHEN ISLAND HANGOUT (`island-`)

**The clubhouse kitchen, grown into a set of its own.** Kitchen **800 W × 580 D × 300 H** + a couch-nook stub west (the clubhouse plan's west couch corner, opened to a wide doorway). Two counter heights by design: **90 worktop** (north side, the cooking face) / **75 hangout ledge** (south face) — the island is a stage with a pit.

**Scenes it exists to host:**
1. **"Chef Hiram's Island Court"** — Hiram behind the island (his bench), six managers on the stools as jury, the pan rail behind him as his gavel rack. Verdicts delivered mid-chop. *Designed feature:* the island as a **counter-divide** — chef elevated on the 90 side, jury at the 75 ledge, CAM B over his shoulder to the row of faces.
2. **"The Stool Summit Breaks Down"** — a negotiation across the island escalates; everything on the island is soft ammunition. *Designed feature:* at 120 wide the island is **grabbable** (the anti-draft-table) — the mess props (bowls, towel, fruit) are placed as the escalation ladder.
3. **"The Couch-Corner Escape"** — someone storms off the stool, through the wide doorway, and flops face-down on the nook couch, still in frame. *Designed feature:* the **150-wide doorway** — CAM D holds island + doorway + couch in one composition; the flop lands without a cut.

**Furniture & dressing:** ① island 320 × 120 (worktop 90 N / ledge 75 S), cream top, honey base ② stools ×6 along the south face ③ range 110 + hood on the north wall ④ pan rail with pans ×5 + ladle above the range (the gavel rack) ⑤ counter run, honey cabinets, full north + east walls ⑥ knife block on the island ⑦ utensil crock ⑧ cutting boards ×2, one mid-chop with vegetables ⑨ mixing bowls ×3 ⑩ frying pan on the island's induction plate ⑪ fruit bowl ⑫ cans ×4 ⑬ dish towel draped over the island edge ⑭ cookbook stand with a blank book ⑮ paper-towel holder ⑯ sink + faucet, east run ⑰ pendant lights ×3 over the island at 215 ⑱ the nook: couch (2), red cushion, floor lamp, rug edge, capybara bed.

**Seating: 6 stools** (seat plane **45** — counter stools at the standard; the 75 ledge is what makes a 45 stool correct) + 2 on the nook couch (45) + standing room for 3–4 in the cooking lane. Full-cast scenes stage 6 seated / rest standing + nook.

**Cameras:** A — FROM COUCH CORNER: in the nook, east through the doorway, island beyond. B — STOOL ROW / JURY: behind the island on the cooking side, south to the six faces. C — CHEF / RANGE: west-center, NE to the range + island corner. D — DOORWAY FLOW: east side, west through the doorway to the couch.

**Sheet notes:** in island-stools the stools sit at a perimeter counter rather than the island — wide + plan are the contract: **stools at the island's south ledge**. Pans in island-range are copper/steel and detailed; builds keep the clubhouse's simplified pan shapes.

## 5. THE OUTDOOR PICKUP COURT (`court-`) — NEW

**Blacktop behind the fence: where arguments get settled one-on-one.** Half-court: blacktop **1400 W × 1000 D**, one hoop (east), chain-link fence all round (300 H) with the **gate at the SW corner**. Markings are flat color inlays: white boundary, navy center circle, red key — the arena's palette, outdoors.

**Scenes it exists to host:**
1. **"One-on-One Grudge Match"** — versus-intro territory outdoors: the challenger enters through the gate (CAM A holds it like a tunnel entrance), loser walks the fence line. *Designed feature:* the **gate as an entrance** + the center circle as the face-off mark (CAM D on the versus axis).
2. **"The Bench Tribunal"** — non-players sit the bench like a panel of judges, cooler as their desk, delivering verdicts between possessions. *Designed feature:* the **bench + cooler corner** on the west fence, long enough for 4 judges + hecklers standing behind the fence.
3. **"Lights-Out Challenge"** — STREETLIGHT state: a shot retaken again and again inside one pool of light while the court sits dark. *Designed feature:* the **two end streetlights make two natural spotlights**; the hoop end is one of them — hero shots need no rig.

**Furniture & dressing:** ① hoop: gooseneck pole, weathered white board 180 × 120 (blank; painted square outline only), rim at **305**, chain net ② center circle r 180, navy ③ key 490 × 580, red ④ bench 380 × 40 along the west fence — the Tribunal ⑤ red cylinder water cooler on a stand at the bench's north end ⑥ cup stacks ×2 (plain) ⑦ basketballs ×3 (bench ×2, one loose at the fence) ⑧ duffel bags ×2 under the bench ⑨ streetlight poles ×3 (NW, N-center, NE) ⑩ chain-link fence + SW gate ⑪ **the puddle** — one permanent sheen patch west of the circle (a running gag: it never dries, shots slip there) ⑫ tree + rooftop silhouette shapes beyond the fence (flat-color, per the foliage rule) ⑬ a bike leaned on the outside of the fence (S side, silhouette dressing).

**Seating: the bench seats 4–5** (seat plane **45** — yes, even outdoors; it's the show's bench) + unlimited standing. Players on the blacktop.

**Lighting variants (both rendered):** GOLDEN HOUR (court-wide, court-hoop) — low warm sun, long shadows, the default. STREETLIGHT NIGHT (court-bench) — deep blue darkness, two warm pools (bench end + hoop end); the tribunal lives in one, the rim in the other.

**Cameras:** A — MASTER: at the gate, SW, court + hoop beyond. B — HOOP CAM: low at the free-throw line, up at board + net. C — TRIBUNAL: SE inside, NW to the bench corner (the night sheet). D — VERSUS AXIS: west-center behind the circle, east down the court.

**Sheet notes:** the nets in the sheets read as cord; **chain net is the build contract** (pickup-court canon). Golden-hour foliage is shape-flat in the wide; keep it that way in the build.

## 6. THE LOCKER ROOM (`locker-`) — NEW

**Postgame territory: celebrations, dejected losses, and the room where trade news lands.** Interior **820 W × 560 D × 300 H**. Locker banks on the west + east walls; whiteboard on the north wall; **double doors (160) centered on the south wall** — the storm-in entrance. In the fiction this room sits off the arena (Step 2b) — same building, same palette, warmer floor.

**Scenes it exists to host:**
1. **"The Presser Nobody Asked For"** — the winner climbs the center bench and holds a press conference for an audience of losers. *Designed feature:* the **center bench as podium** — double-sided, mid-room, with the **presser floor** (open space) between it and the whiteboard for the "media" to stand in.
2. **"The Dejected Row"** — after a loss: four managers slumped in a line on the bench, towels over heads, lockers as backrests. *Designed feature:* the **long bench + hook row** — hooks above the bench hold the towels that become the towels-over-heads; CAM C runs the row.
3. **"Trade News Drops"** — the whole room crowds the whiteboard as Muse writes the news (production-textured) and the room erupts; someone storms in through the double doors with the update. *Designed feature:* the **whiteboard wall as the gathering focus** + the 160 doors opposite — entrance and reveal in one CAM A frame.

**Furniture & dressing:** ① locker banks ×2 (W + E), each 10 units 44 W × 210 H: navy frames, cream doors, vent slats, **all doors blank — no names, no numbers, ever** ② benches in front of both banks, 440 long ③ center bench 300 × 80, double-sided ④ whiteboard plane 260 × 120 (blank), center 170, north wall ⑤ towels — draped on benches, hanging on the hook row, folded stacks (white + navy) ⑥ plain jersey shapes ×2 on hooks (navy, red — garments, no numbers) ⑦ duffel bags ×5 under benches ⑧ sneaker pairs ×4 on the floor ⑨ water bottles ×6 (benches + floor) ⑩ laundry bins ×2 (S corners) ⑪ one gold trophy on top of the west bank (last season's; slightly dusty is a shading note, not a prop) ⑫ a plain boombox on the center bench's north end (celebration music source; shape only, no brand) ⑬ foam roller leaning by the east bank ⑭ ice bucket beside the center bench (for the winner's presser, obviously).

**Seating: benches seat ~14** in a squeeze (bank benches 5 + 5, center bench 4 across both sides) — all at the **45** standard. A full-cast postgame fits seated; the presser speaker stands ON the center bench (fiction load-bearing, noted for the rig team).

**Cameras:** A — MASTER: at/outside the double doors, room + whiteboard in one frame. B — BOARD / PRESSER: south-center, north to the board over the podium. C — BENCH ROW: low at the west bench's south end, down the dejected row. D — DEJECTED ROW (reverse): NE corner, SW across both benches.

**Sheet notes:** locker-wide's foreground renders the double doors oddly (they read as a flat panel at the camera's feet — a sheet artifact); **locker-board is the clean master composition**, and the doors live on the south wall per the plan. Locker doors in all sheets are correctly blank — hold that line in the build (no nameplates).
