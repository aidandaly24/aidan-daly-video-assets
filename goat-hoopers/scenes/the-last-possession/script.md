# THE LAST POSSESSION — GOAT Hoopers sitcom pilot scene (AUDIO)

**Setting:** The Clubhouse, game night. The whole league is on the couch watching the
Game of the Week. Final possession: Aidan leads Phil by 0.4 fantasy points, Phil's
player has the ball, nine seconds left. Aidan loses — to a garbage-time free throw.

**Target runtime:** 2:15–2:45 · **Dialogue:** ~375 words · **Structure:** setup → bits → whisper gag → climax → button

## SCRIPT

**[MUSIC: intro sting — brassy sitcom transition, 8s. Cold open straight off it.]**

**01 · AIDAN** *(center-left, urgent, hushed)*
"All right, everybody shut up. Final possession of the Game of the Week. I am up four-tenths of a point on Phil, his guy has the ball, and there are nine seconds left. Nobody breathe, nobody move, nobody jinx this."

**02 · KEARNS** *(left, deadpan)*
"For the record, Aidan has been up four-tenths of a point for eleven straight minutes. The lead has a mailing address now. It's receiving packages."

**03 · PHIL** *(far right, emperor bluster)*
"Four-tenths of a point. I tip more than that at brunch. The emperor does not lose a Game of the Week to a decimal."

**04 · GRIFFIN** *(far left, bright)*
"Hey, quick thing — in my game, I actually had a guy go for thirty—"

**05 · VANN** *(center-right, flat)*
"Griffin."

**06 · GRIFFIN** *(self-aware, cheerful)*
"Right. Zero and six. My season ended in October. Please, continue."

*[On TV: Aidan's center hauls down a defensive rebound.]*

**07 · TYRESE** *(right, slow, biblical)*
"Behold. A giant rises above mortal men... and secures a defensive rebound. Somewhere, a statue is being commissioned."

**08 · HIRAM** *(kitchen, rear-right, calling over)*
"Wings have got ninety seconds left. If this goes to overtime, I am eating every single one in front of you."

**09 · TOMMY** *(couch)*
"I dropped Phil's guy in week two. Week two. He has done this to me in my sleep ever since."

**10 · MUSE** *(by the TV, dry)*
"I have run the final numbers eleven times. Statistically, someone in this room is about to have a very bad night."

*[On TV: Phil's star limps toward the bench. SFX: phone buzz on the coffee table.]*

**11 · AIDAN**
"Wait, wait. Their star is limping. Lax, you have the injury report open — your phone just lit up. Is he coming back in, or is he done?"

*[LORD LAX leans in and WHISPERS into Vann's ear. Nobody can make out a word.]*

**12 · LORD LAX** *(whisper SFX — inaudible)*

**13 · VANN** *(translating, solemn)*
"He says... Aidan should trade Giannis to Vann. Straight up. Tonight. Out of respect for the game."

**14 · AIDAN**
"Vann. He did not say that. He has never said that. Nobody has ever said that."

**15 · VANN** *(admitting it)*
"Okay, fine. Real translation: he's probable to return. He is coming back in, Aidan. I am so sorry."

*[On TV: the star checks back in and pulls up for the final shot.]*

**16 · AIDAN** *(rising panic)*
"He's back in. Of course he's back in. Here he comes — he's pulling up—"

**[SFX: CROWD ERUPTS — the shot misses.]**

**17 · AIDAN** *(shouting over the crowd, premature)*
"HE MISSED! That's it! I win the Game of the—"

**18 · KEARNS** *(deadpan, quiet)*
"There's a whistle."

*[Beat. A foul away from the ball. A nobody is going to the line.]*

**19 · TYRESE** *(slow, doom-laden)*
"A whistle, in the dying breath of battle... sends a forgotten man to the free-throw line. One shot. Worth... everything."

**[SFX: CROWD ERUPTS, smaller — the free throw drops. Phil wins by 0.6.]**

**20 · PHIL** *(triumphant)*
"Count it. Phil, by six-tenths of a point. The emperor thanks you all for attending his coronation."

**21 · AIDAN** *(hollow)*
"I lost... to a garbage-time free throw. The game was over. That free throw was ceremonial."

**[MUSIC: fail sting — sad brass, 4s.]**

**22 · MUSE** *(dry closer)*
"Final: Phil, by six-tenths of a point. Aidan — the decimal sends its regards."

**[MUSIC: outro sting — playful resolving brass, 6s. End.]**
